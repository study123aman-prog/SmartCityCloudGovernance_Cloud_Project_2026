# Geography & Spatial Corridor Data

This directory references geographic wildfire corridors and digital topography used by FireGuard AI's Leaflet Geospatial Risk engine.

## Monitored Regional Basins
- **Sierra Foothills Corridor** (38.5439°N, -120.9380°W, elev: 680m, vegetation: Dry Pine & Chaparral)
- **Cascades Valley Basin** (45.6298°N, -121.8535°W, elev: 420m, vegetation: Douglas Fir & Cedar)
- **Pacific Coast Ridge** (38.2620°N, -122.1024°W, elev: 310m, vegetation: Oak Woodland)
- **High Desert Plateau** (34.6882°N, -117.2833°W, elev: 950m, vegetation: Sagebrush & Juniper Scrub)
- **Redwood Coastal Basin** (41.2033°N, -124.0046°W, elev: 150m, vegetation: Coastal Redwood)
- **Angeles National Forest Ridge** (34.2500°N, -118.1500°W, elev: 1200m, vegetation: Dense Chaparral)

Topological coordinates and perimeter polygons are rendered live via open-source Leaflet map layers in `frontend/src/pages/FireMap.jsx`.
