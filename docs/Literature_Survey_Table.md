### Overview

The following comparative matrix analyzes foundational research papers and prominent open-source repositories across five core domains:
1. **IoT & Edge-Based Fire Detection Hardware Systems**
2. **Machine Learning & Deep Learning Fire Hazard Classifiers**
3. **Spatial Hazard Modeling & Digital Twin Building Simulations**
4. **Dynamic Evacuation Pathfinding & Algorithmic Graph Egress Routing**
5. **Cloud-Native Emergency Response Architectures & Multi-Source Risk Fusion**

Each entry details the methodology, dataset/platform used, key advantages, inherent limitations, and the specific research/engineering gap addressed by **IgnisCore — Intelligent Fire Hazard Monitoring System**.

### Key Synthesis & Technical Positioning

As demonstrated across the 15 surveyed works, existing systems either:
1. Focus narrowly on **hardware sensors** that fail under fire conditions (`GrumpyKit10`, `boldmonk89`).
2. Focus strictly on **wildfire flame detection** while ignoring indoor facilities (`FireShield360`, `Forest-Sentinel`).
3. Focus on **heavy numerical physics simulations** that cannot run in real time (`Wu et al.`, `Mell et al.`).
4. Treat the cloud as a **passive data pipe** without predictive risk intelligence or dynamic evacuation routing (`Patel et al.`).

**IgnisCore — Intelligent Fire Hazard Monitoring System** directly bridges these divides by integrating **software-only virtual multi-zone telemetry**, **dual-domain machine learning + rule explainability**, **algorithmic graph hazard propagation**, **dynamic Dijkstra safest evacuation routing**, **interactive What-If simulation**, and a **decoupled local-first / AWS event-driven architecture**.
